Two traps in any Trends data

  1. Per-request scaling. Google scales every request so its top term is 100. Numbers from two requests can't be put side by side, and a small term next to a big one rounds to 0. The fix is to chain overlapping groups through a shared keyword: how it works.
  2. Market-wide glitches. From late March to mid-June 2026, small, unrelated US searches all ran about 2x their usual level at once, then snapped back. Growth rates and peak dates for long-tail terms across that window were inflated. The check is a basket of unrelated control terms, and we run it every morning: Is Google Trends glitching today?

FAQ

Is there an official Google Trends API? Only an alpha with gated access, announced in July 2025. Apply through Google's Trends API page.

Why do I get 429 errors? Google rate-limits Trends and doesn't publish the limits. In our experience, bursts of requests and shared cloud IPs hit them first; retrying from a fresh IP after a pause usually works.

Can I compare more than 5 keywords? Not in one request on the website. Chain overlapping groups through a shared keyword, or use a tool that does it for you.
