Report · October 2026

Small US searches doubled on Google Trends this spring. Demand didn't.

We tracked 20 everyday US searches in four groups (household, hobby, auto, pets and garden) against the same week of 2025, so seasonality cancels out. From late March to late June 2026 they ran 1.5 to 2.2 times their 2025 level, all at the same time. Big searches like weather, gmail and translate stayed flat. By August it was gone.

What it means

  • Growth for long-tail terms that compares spring 2026 with any other period is inflated.
  • Peak dates between late March and late June 2026 are suspect.
  • Big head terms look fine.

Check your own terms

Pull a handful of unrelated control terms of similar size next to yours. If the controls jump together, the jump is Google's data, not your keyword. Our Google Trends tool runs this check on every run and flags the dates for you.
