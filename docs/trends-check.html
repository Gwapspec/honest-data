How it works

  • The basket: shower curtain, doormat, can opener, dish rack, ice cube tray. These are small, everyday US searches that have nothing to do with each other.
  • The level: each term is divided by its own usual (median) value, and the line is the middle of the five, smoothed over 3 points. 1.0 means normal.
  • A jump: a level of 1.4x or more. Shaded periods are jumps.
  • Spring 2026: the basket ran 1.5 to 2.2 times its 2025 level from late March to mid-June, while big terms like weather and gmail stayed flat. Growth rates and peak dates for small terms across that window are inflated. The research, with weekly data (CC BY).
  • Limits: Google samples its data, and daily numbers for small terms are noisy, so a one- or two-day blip may be sampling. English-language US searches only.
