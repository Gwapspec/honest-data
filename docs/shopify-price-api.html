Shopify product prices API: how to read store prices and check discounts

You can read the prices of most Shopify and WooCommerce stores without an account. Here is what each option gives you, and what none of them can tell you.

Your options

OptionAccessCoversNotes
Store's own /products.json (Shopify)Free, no login, up to 250 products per pageAny Shopify store that leaves it onOpen JSON with variants, price, compare-at price and an availability flag per variant. Stores can switch it off or password-protect the shop. Prices are in the store's default currency; the feed doesn't state the currency (/meta.json does).
WooCommerce Store API (/wp-json/wc/store/v1/products)Free, no login, up to 100 products per pageWooCommerce stores that leave the public Store API onPrices come as whole numbers in the currency's smallest unit (1999 = 19.99), with the currency code in the same response.
Shopify Admin or Storefront APINeeds the store owner's access tokenYour own store, or a partner'sThe right tool for your own shop. It can't read a competitor's.
Hosted scrapers, e.g. on Apify StorePay per use, no setupDepends on the toolRun in the cloud, deal with rate limits and paging. Most charge about $0.001 per product; check whether they report discounts and changes.

What a price feed can't tell you

  1. The compare-at price is typed in by the store. A "was $50, now $25" tag only means the field says 50. Compare the same product across weeks and stores before you call it a deal.
  2. The feed doesn't say how long a sale has run. updated_at moves on any edit, so it isn't a sale start. The only way to know is to read the store on a schedule and keep what you saw.

FAQ

Is there an official way to read another store's prices? No. Shopify's APIs need that store's token; the public feeds above are what stores publish themselves.

Why does a store return HTTP 429? Some stores rate-limit or block datacenter IPs. We saw bombas.com refuse its product feed in 4 of 5 runs, even through residential IPs; others, like allbirds.com and gymshark.com, answered freely.

Can it show when a sale started? Not from one run. Schedule it and compare runs.
